Rail

The Interrail pass — continuous, and why 15 days is enough

Each traveller carries one Interrail Global Pass — 15 days continuous, 2nd class. It doesn't have to span the whole ~19-day trip because there's no train travel on the first two days or the last three:

Dec 18–19
Arrive + Munich — no pass
Dec 20 → Jan 3
15 continuous days — pass active
Jan 4–6
Frankfurt + fly out — no pass

Activate on leaving Munich (Dec 20). Day 1 is Dec 20; day 15 is Jan 3 — which still covers the Lucerne → Basel → Strasbourg → Frankfurt travel day. Every rail day falls inside the window:

  • Dec 20 Munich → Nuremberg · Dec 22 Nuremberg → Prague · Dec 25 Prague → Vienna
  • Dec 26 Bratislava day trip · Dec 27→28 Nightjet Vienna → Zurich · Dec 28 Grindelwald day trip
  • Dec 30 Zurich → Lucerne · Lucerne day trips · Jan 3 Lucerne → Strasbourg → Frankfurt

Seat reservations and the Nightjet compartment are a separate cost (line item above) and are mandatory on the Nightjet, Railjet, ICE and the French trains.
