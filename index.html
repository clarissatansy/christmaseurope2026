01

Munich

December 15–17 · 2 nights
🎄 Christmas market open

Bavaria's capital — a proper first stop rather than just a landing pad, with its own centuries-old Christkindlmarkt at Marienplatz and a walkable old town to shake off jet lag in.

  • Christkindlmarkt am Marienplatz — over 130 stalls beneath the Neues Rathaus, with Advent music from the town hall balcony
  • Frauenkirche and the old town — the twin-domed skyline that defines Munich
  • Purpose-built as a proper first stop, not just a transit buffer — both markets and old-town wandering fit comfortably in 2 days

Nuremberg

December 17–19 · 2 nights
🎄 Christmas market open

Germany's most famous Christmas market, running in some form since the 1600s — plus a walled medieval old town built for wandering with a paper cone of roasted almonds.

  • Christkindlesmarkt — Germany's most famous Christmas market
  • Medieval Old Town, Nuremberg Castle, and the surviving city walls
  • Local specialties: original Nuremberg bratwurst and lebkuchen (gingerbread)

Prague

December 19–22 · 3 nights
🎄 Christmas market open

The fairy-tale midpoint of the trip — market stalls in Old Town Square, Charles Bridge, and golden-hour views over the Vltava, all at full, unhurried hours since you're through well before Christmas Eve.

  • Old Town Square Christmas Market — full normal hours throughout this stay
  • Charles Bridge, Prague Castle, and golden-hour views over the Vltava
  • The Astronomical Clock and the tangle of Old Town lanes around it

04

Vienna

December 22–24 · 2 nights + sleeper
🎄 Christmas market open

Three full days in Vienna — two in a hotel, the third rolled into the evening you board the Nightjet — with enough room for both Schönbrunn and Belvedere without racing between them, both signature markets at full hours, and proper time for Vienna's classic café culture.

  • Rathausplatz and Schönbrunn Christmas markets — both running at full hours during this stay
  • Schönbrunn and Belvedere Palaces — enough time to actually do both, not just one
  • Vienna's classic café culture, the Ringstrasse, and Stephansdom in the old centre
  • Nightjet sleeper departs the evening of Dec 24 — pack an overnight bag separately

The Signature Segment

ÖBB Nightjet — Vienna to Zurich

The overnight sleeper that turns a transit day into part of the trip itself: fall asleep in Austria on Christmas Eve, wake up in Switzerland on Christmas Day morning, via Innsbruck.

  • TrainÖBB Nightjet, Vienna → Zurich, via Innsbruck
  • ClassPrivate sleeper compartment (1–3 people) — Standard (own washbasin) or Deluxe (private shower/toilet)
  • Departs~21:30–21:40 on Dec 24 (Christmas Eve), arriving ~07:50–10:30 Christmas Day morning (~11–13h)
  • PricingStandard sleeper from ~€89/person; Deluxe runs higher — compare both closer to booking

05

Zurich

December 25–26 · 1 night
Christmas markets closed by this date

A single overnight on Christmas Day — arriving by sleeper into Switzerland's alpine-and-lake half of the trip, with just enough time for a quiet lake walk before moving on to Lucerne the next morning.

  • Lake Zurich — an easy shoreline walk with the Alps on the horizon; the natural fit for one evening here
  • Uetliberg and the Rhine Falls (Rheinfall) are both reachable from Zurich, but with only one night, treat them as optional rather than firm plans — Rheinfall works just as well as a day trip from Lucerne given the short hop back through Zurich
  • Christmas markets are closed by this date — see the market status below
Lake ZurichUetliberg (optional)Rhine Falls (optional)
06

Lucerne

December 26–29 · 3 nights
Christmas markets closed by this date

The finale — mountains and lake instead of markets, with Rigi, Pilatus, and a lakeside cruise closing out the trip before the journey home. Both of Lucerne's Christmas markets end before arrival, but the Chapel Bridge is still there in town when you want it.

  • Mount Pilatus — reachable by the world's steepest cogwheel railway, with winter alpine views over the lake
  • Rigi, the "Queen of the Mountains" — cable car and cogwheel train up, often above the cloud line
  • Lake Lucerne (Vierwaldstättersee) — a steamer cruise past the four forested cantons that ring the lake

Eat Your Way Through It

Market food & drink

What's actually worth queueing for at each stop.

Munich · Nuremberg · Prague · Vienna

Glühwein

Hot mulled wine, spiced with cinnamon and citrus and served in a souvenir mug — the drink that defines every German-speaking market.

Munich

Weisswurst & Pretzel

Munich's classic pairing — a soft Laugenbrezel alongside white veal sausage with sweet mustard, traditionally a late-morning snack.

Nuremberg

Original Nürnberger Bratwurst

Small, finger-sized grilled sausages, traditionally served drei im weckla — three in a bread roll — a Nuremberg specialty since medieval times.

Nuremberg · Vienna

Lebkuchen(herzen)

Nuremberg's iconic soft gingerbread, often iced and sold as decorated heart-shaped ornaments strung with ribbon.

Prague

Trdelník

A sweet rolled pastry dough wrapped around a spit, cooked over open coals, then rolled in sugar and cinnamon — a Prague Old Town street-food staple.

Vienna · Munich

Roasted Chestnuts

Maroni — hot roasted chestnuts sold from charcoal braziers, a classic hand-warmer-and-snack combination at Austrian and German markets.

Prague

Trdelník, over the coals

Watching the dough turn golden over an open flame is half the appeal of the stalls around Old Town Square.

Nuremberg

Lebkuchen, classic style

The plain spiced-honey version, dense and chewy, sold by weight from market stalls piled high through December.
