01

Frankfurt

December 19 · 1 night
🎄 Christmas market open

The arrival city — a soft landing after two long-haul flights, with just enough daylight to shake off jet lag among half-timbered houses and mulled wine.

  • Römerberg Christmas Market — one of Germany's oldest, in the medieval old town square
  • Old Town (Römer) — half-timbered houses and the historic city hall
  • Purpose-built as an arrival / settling-in day, nothing overscheduled
Römerberg MarketOld TownArrival Day
02

Nuremberg

December 20–21 · 2 nights
🎄 Christmas market open

Germany's most famous Christmas market, running in some form since the 1600s — plus a walled medieval old town built for wandering with a paper cone of roasted almonds.

  • Christkindlesmarkt — Germany's most famous Christmas market
  • Medieval Old Town, Nuremberg Castle, and the surviving city walls
  • Local specialties: original Nuremberg bratwurst and lebkuchen (gingerbread)
ChristkindlesmarktNuremberg CastleBratwurstLebkuchen
03

Prague

December 22–24 · 3 nights
🎄 Christmas market open

Christmas Eve here lands on Štědrý večer, the main event of Czech Christmas — family celebration night — set against Charles Bridge and golden-hour views over the Vltava.

  • Old Town Square Christmas Market, running through Dec 24 (shortened hours that day)
  • Christmas Eve (Štědrý večer) — the main Czech family celebration night
  • Charles Bridge, Prague Castle, and golden-hour views over the Vltava
Old Town Square MarketChristmas EveCharles BridgeTrdelník
04

Vienna

December 25–26 · 2 nights
🎄 Christmas market open

A trimmed two-night stay that covers Christmas Day and Boxing Day only — grand palaces, classic Viennese café culture, and two markets still running over the holiday itself.

  • Rathausplatz and Schönbrunn Christmas markets — both still running over Christmas
  • Palaces (Schönbrunn, Belvedere) and Vienna's classic café culture
  • NYE happens later in Lucerne, not here, in this version of the route

The Signature Segment

ÖBB Nightjet — Vienna to Zurich

The overnight sleeper that turns a transit day into part of the trip itself: fall asleep in Austria, wake up in Switzerland via Innsbruck.

  • TrainÖBB Nightjet, Vienna → Zurich, via Innsbruck
  • ClassPrivate sleeper compartment (1–3 people) — Standard (own washbasin) or Deluxe (private shower/toilet)
  • Departs~21:30–21:40, arriving ~07:50–10:30 the next morning (~11–13h)
  • PricingStandard sleeper from ~€89/person; Deluxe runs higher — compare both closer to booking
  • IncludedWelcome package, breakfast delivered to your compartment, Wi-Fi
  • BookingTickets go on sale ~6 months ahead and can sell out on peak holiday dates — book as soon as they open
  • Rolling stockNewer-generation Nightjet scheduled on this route by these travel dates — updated cabins expected
05

Zurich

December 27–28 · 2 nights
Christmas markets closed by this date

Arriving fresh off the sleeper into Switzerland's alpine-and-lake half of the trip. Markets have wrapped up for the season here, so this leg trades market stalls for mountain air and moving water — with the Old Town still there for an evening stroll.

  • Uetliberg — Zurich's own mountain, a ~20-minute train ride to panoramic Alps-and-lake views and forest hiking trails
  • Day trip to the Rhine Falls (Rheinfall) — the largest waterfall in Europe, about 50 minutes by train
  • Lake Zurich — walk or cruise the shoreline with the Alps on the horizon
  • Christmas markets are closed by this date — see the market status below
UetlibergRhine FallsLake Zurich
06

Lucerne

December 29–31 · 3 nights
Christmas markets closed by this date

The finale: New Year's Eve on the lake. Both of Lucerne's Christmas markets end before arrival, so this stretch is built around the mountains and the water instead — the Chapel Bridge is still there in town when you want it.

  • Mount Pilatus — reachable by the world's steepest cogwheel railway, with winter alpine views over the lake
  • Rigi, the "Queen of the Mountains" — cable car and cogwheel train up, often above the cloud line
  • Lake Lucerne (Vierwaldstättersee) — a steamer cruise past the four forested cantons that ring the lake
  • New Year's Eve on the lake — Lucerne's own lakeside NYE atmosphere

Eat Your Way Through It

Market food & drink

What's actually worth queueing for at each stop.

Frankfurt · Nuremberg · Prague · Vienna

Glühwein

Hot mulled wine, spiced with cinnamon and citrus and served in a souvenir mug — the drink that defines every German-speaking market.

Nuremberg

Original Nürnberger Bratwurst

Small, finger-sized grilled sausages, traditionally served drei im weckla — three in a bread roll — a Nuremberg specialty since medieval times.

Nuremberg · Vienna

Lebkuchen(herzen)

Nuremberg's iconic soft gingerbread, often iced and sold as decorated heart-shaped ornaments strung with ribbon.

Prague

Trdelník

A sweet rolled pastry dough wrapped around a spit, cooked over open coals, then rolled in sugar and cinnamon — a Prague Old Town street-food staple.

Vienna · Frankfurt

Roasted Chestnuts

Maroni — hot roasted chestnuts sold from charcoal braziers, a classic hand-warmer-and-snack combination at Austrian and German markets.

Prague

Trdelník, over the coals

Watching the dough turn golden over an open flame is half the appeal of the stalls around Old Town Square.

Frankfurt · Nuremberg · Prague · Vienna

Glühwein, in the making

Wine mulled slowly with orange, cloves, and cinnamon before it's ladled out to warm hands between market stalls.

Nuremberg

Lebkuchen, classic style

The plain spiced-honey version, dense and chewy, sold by weight from market stalls piled high through December.
